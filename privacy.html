Privacy Policy

Chains – a cozy number-chain puzzle

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Chains app ("the app") collects, why, and what you can do about it. The app is made by an individual developer, [YOUR FULL NAME], based in Norway, who is responsible for the processing of your personal data. You can reach me at alfredtoget@gmail.com.

In short: the app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos. It stores your game progress on your device, keeps a random player ID with a generated nickname and your daily points for the leaderboard, and shows ads from Google AdMob.

1. Information stored on your device

Your level, settings (sound, rain and sum helper), daily challenge status and today's points are saved only on your device. This data never leaves your phone and is deleted if you delete the app.

2. Leaderboard

To place you on the daily leaderboard, the app creates an anonymous account the first time you go online. No email, password or real name is involved. The following is stored on our server:

  • a random player ID
  • your nickname, which is generated by the app (for example "Brave Otter") and can only be chosen from a fixed list
  • your points, the leaderboard pool you are placed in, and when you submitted points, for each day you play

Other players can see your nickname and your points for the day. They cannot see anything else about you.

Why: to run the leaderboard you ask to take part in, and to prevent cheating (for example by limiting how often points can be submitted).

Where: the data is stored with Supabase, on servers in the EU (Ireland).

How long: until you delete it. You can delete everything at any time in the app under Settings → Delete my online data. This removes your nickname, your points and the anonymous account from the server immediately.

3. Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a short ad between levels, and optional ads you can choose to watch for a hint or an extra life.

Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, device type and how you interact with ads, in order to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.

  • In Norway, the EU, the EEA and the UK, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. If you do not consent, you will see non-personalised ads.
  • On iPhone, Apple asks whether the app may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared, and ads are not personalised based on your activity in other apps.

You can change these choices later in your iPhone settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). Read more in Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • It does not sell your personal data.
  • It does not have in-app purchases or chat.

5. Children

Chains is a general puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the leaderboard, use Settings → Delete my online data, or contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to object to its use, and to withdraw consent. The fastest way to delete your leaderboard data is Settings → Delete my online data in the app. For anything else, email alfredtoget@gmail.com.

If you are unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet, or the data protection authority where you live.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
